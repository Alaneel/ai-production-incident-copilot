No node_modules or Python dependency source is vendored into the release tree. Included third-party Go material is limited to the declared generated Collector scaffold, gosu source and modified `moby/sys/user` fixture listed above; their upstream notices are retained. Other Go dependencies are resolved from the frozen module locks, rather than vendored. React, Next.js, vinext, Vite, Cloudflare tooling, LangGraph, FastAPI, Psycopg and other installed dependencies retain the licenses in their distributed packages. The dependency locks and [installed npm license inventory](LICENSES/npm-install-license-inventory.json) and [Python distribution inventory](LICENSES/python-install-license-inventory.json) identify the exact resolved distributions; a binary/container redistribution must preserve applicable package/OS notices as well.

The owner has explicitly confirmed public-release rights for all first-party source and selected MIT. No employer/customer/coauthor restriction is inferred from directory names or Git authorship metadata, and no additional collaborators are invented by this notice.

Psycopg distributions retain [LGPL terms from the exact upstream release](https://github.com/psycopg/psycopg/blob/3.3.4/LICENSE.txt). Source-only publication of this first-party repository does not relicense those packages; any separately authorized binary/container redistribution must preserve their distributed notices and meet applicable corresponding-source/relinking requirements. Local builds retain the package license files under their distribution metadata. Redis, PostgreSQL, observability images and the base operating systems likewise retain upstream terms; their image distributions are outside the first-party MIT grant.
